# City score rubric (0–5)

Every place gets 0–5 in nine categories. Score what a visitor actually experiences, not what merely exists.

Common meaning of each level:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Nationally iconic — people choose this place *because* of it |
| 4 | One of the place's main draws; most visitors do it |
| 3 | Worth putting in the plan |
| 2 | Nice if you have time |
| 1 | A little |
| 0 | Basically none |

Category anchors (what 5 / 4 / 3 look like):

| Category | 5 | 4 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| History 역사 | Ancient capital or UNESCO heritage that defines the trip (Gyeongju, Seoul palaces, Suwon Hwaseong, Haeinsa) | A major heritage site is central to the visit (Jeonju Gyeonggijeon, Ganghwa dolmens) | A notable heritage stop |
| Nature 자연 | Landscape of national-park or world-heritage class (Seoraksan, Jeju, Ulleungdo, Juwangsan) | Nature is a main draw (Suncheon Bay, Danyang) | A good nature spot to include |
| Food 음식 | Food alone is a reason to go (Jeonju, Busan, Seoul, Tongyeong) | Several signature dishes or a famous market | One well-known local dish |
| Café 카페 | A café destination in itself (Seoul, Gangneung, Jeju) | Notable café streets | Some nice cafés |
| Photo 사진 | Instantly recognisable, iconic shots | Many photogenic spots | A few good photo spots |
| Calm 여유 | Slow and quiet; healing is the point | Mostly calm, few crowds | Calm corners if you look |
| Walking 걷기 | Hiking/activity destination (Seoraksan, Hallasan, Ulleungdo) | Significant hiking, trails or surfing | A good walk |
| Art 예술 | A major museum and gallery scene (Seoul) | A nationally notable art museum (MMCA Cheongju) | Some galleries or art villages |
| Sea 바다 | The sea is the main event (Busan, Jeju, Yeosu, Tongyeong) | Coastal, with strong sea attractions | Sea views or a beach nearby |
